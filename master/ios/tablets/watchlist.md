# Watchlist

The Watchlist screen is accessible by default after logging in to the application.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (840).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

In the Watchlist you can find information by traded symbols:

* Last price – the last trade price;
* Change, % – the price deviation from current day's opening price in a percentage.

In order to get full symbol information and open the chart, tap the Symbol name. Tap the button![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (841).png>)at the top right corner of the Chart to open the Info window:

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (842).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Volume – the total amount traded today;
* Open – the day's open price;
* High – the day's highest price;
* Low – the day's lowest price;
* Previous close – the previous day's close price.
* Funding (optional) – current funding rate value as a percentage including broker tax;
* Countdown (optional) – displays the countdown time to the next funding settlement.

### &#xD;**Indices**

The Indices section provides information about the index price for the recent period as a chart and has the following view:

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (843).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Above the chart there is:

* Index name - the name of the index;
* Last price – the last trade price;
* Change, % – the price deviation from current day's opening price in a percentage.

It is possible to select the required Time frame for the chart in the card:

* 1d - Day;
* 1w - Week;
* 1m - Month;
* 3m - 3 months;
* 6m - 6 months;
* 1y - Year.

By tapping on the card, it will give more detailed information about the asset:

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (844).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

It contains the next indicators:

* Open – the day's open price;
* Change, % – the price deviation from current day's opening price in a percentage.
* High – the day's highest price;
* Low – the day's lowest price;
* Previous close – the previous day's close price;
* Volume – the total amount traded today.

### &#xD;**Symbol lookup**&#x20;

In order to open Symbol lookup, tap the Search field. Check/uncheck the desired symbols. Selected symbols will automatically appear in the Watchlist.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (845).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Swipe left the selected symbol to open Market depth/Order entry/Chart screen by this instrument.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (846).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### **Symbols list creation**

The Watchlist screen enables a user to create custom sets of symbols lists. In order to add symbols to the Watchlist, tap the![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (848).png>)button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (847).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Select the needed symbols from the 'Symbols lookup' window. Tap the "Create new" button to create new symbol lists. Buttons for list renaming or deleting are located next to each list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (849).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### **Watchlist Filter**&#x20;

In order to filter the list of displayed symbols tap the button<img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (851).png" alt="" data-size="line">,&#x20;located at the top right corner of the Symbol lookup. Choose the needed exchange from the list of available.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (850).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### &#xD;**Symbol menu**

Swipe left the symbol name to open the Symbol menu toolbar.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (852).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Market depth – opens the level 2 quotes for the selected symbol;
* Order Entry – opens the Order entry screen for the selected symbol;
* Remove – allows removing the Symbol from the Watchlist.
